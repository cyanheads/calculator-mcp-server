# 0.6.0 — 2026-11-12

## Added

- **Date functions** `dateDiff`, `dateAdd`, `weekday`, `isoWeek`, and `daysInMonth` take quoted `YYYY-MM-DD` dates (years 0000–9999) and the units `"days"` (default), `"weeks"`, `"months"`, `"years"`; month and year steps clamp to the end of the month ([#51](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/51)).
- **`integrate(f, x, a, b)`** (alias `integral`) estimates a definite integral over finite bounds with adaptive Gauss–Kronrod quadrature, up to 500 subintervals; `numericType` `"number"` only ([#52](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/52)).
- **Operator spellings** `**`, `×`, `÷`, `−`, `°`, `°C`, and `°F` read as `^`, `*`, `/`, `-`, `deg`, `degC`, and `degF` in every operation, outside string literals and `#` comments; a vector or matrix operand of `×` or `**` fails with `type_mismatch` ([#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/47)).
- **`eigs`, `polynomialRoot`, and `lusolve`** are named in the tool description, `eig` and `eigenvalues(A)` resolve, and a call to `roots` or `solve` fails with `parse_failed` and a pointer to `polynomialRoot` and `lusolve` that ends `Run either with operation "evaluate".` ([#48](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/48)).
- **Exact Fraction combinatorics and roots**: `factorial` / `!`, `permutations`, `combinations`, `gamma` at positive integers, `catalan`, `composition`, and `multinomial` return exact integers (`10!` → `3628800/1`); `sqrt`, `cbrt`, `nthRoot`, and `^` with a rational exponent are exact when the result is (`sqrt(9/4)` → `3/2`). A result past 1,000,000 digits fails with `result_too_large` before computing ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **Fraction `nthRoot` takes collections**, elementwise as in number mode: `nthRoot([8, 27/8], 3)` → `[2/1, 3/2]`, `nthRoot(16, [2, 4])` → `[4/1, 2/1]`, `nthRoot([4, 9])` → `[2/1, 3/1]`; an irrational element fails as `sqrt(2)` does ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **Complex powers and roots under Fraction** are exact when their parts are integers (`(1+2i)^2` → `-3 + 4i`) ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **`calculator-guide` skill** at `skill://calculator-guide/SKILL.md`, served through `createApp({ skills })`: numericType selection, what a result does not prove, and workflows for derivatives, dates, statistics, and units.

## Changed

- **Scope values under numericType BigNumber** are read as BigNumbers, like literals: a non-integer scope value passed to `gamma`, `!`, `erf`, or `lgamma` now fails as the literal does (`gamma(x)` with `{"x": 0.5}` returned `1.7724538509055154`, now `evaluation_failed`) ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **Scope values keep their digits**: under BigNumber `(x+y)-x` with `{"x": 1e16, "y": 1}` returns `1` (was `0`), `x` returns a BigNumber, and `x + 1` with 20 digits no longer fails with `type_mismatch`; under Fraction a scope value reads as its shortest decimal (`{"x": 1e23}` → `100000000000000000000000/1`) ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **`nthRoots(x, n)`, `rightLogShift(x, y)`, and `fraction(x, y)`** reject scope values under BigNumber, as they reject BigNumber literals ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **`zeta` under BigNumber** fails with `evaluation_failed`, in any form (`zeta(x)` with `{"x": 2}`, `zeta(0)`, `zeta(bignumber(2))`): math.js computes it to about 7 digits at that precision; use `"number"` ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **`stirlingS2` and `bellNumbers` above n = 1,412 (314 with a BigNumber argument) and `bernoulli` above n = 1,000** fail with `result_too_large`.
- **A `range` of more than 1,000,000 elements** fails with `result_too_large`, as other collections past the per-call limit already did: `count(range(0, 1e6 - 1e-7))` returned `1000001` ([#43](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/43)).
- **Identifiers containing `×` or `÷`** no longer parse: `a×b + 1` with `{"a×b": 5}` returned `6`, now `parse_failed` ([#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/47)).
- **`avg`, `average`, `stdev`, and `stddev` read a dimension one-based**, like `mean` and `std`: `avg([1,2;3,4], 1)` returns `[2, 3]` (was `[1.5, 3.5]`), and `avg([1,2;3,4], 0)` fails with `evaluation_failed` (was `[2, 3]`) ([#42](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/42)).
- **`now` and `today` calls fail under `evaluate`**, including calls to a function the expression defines with either name, with a hint to pass a quoted date; `simplify` and `derivative` still treat them as symbolic ([#51](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/51)).
- **Under Fraction, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `sign`, and `fix` of an irrational power** (`floor(2^(1/2))`) and a complex-zero exponent (`4^(0i + 1/2)`) fail with `fraction_unsupported`, like `floor(sqrt(2))` ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **Nesting that exhausts the call stack fails with `expression_too_deep`**, a new declared reason, whether the stack runs out while parsing, walking, or evaluating the expression, instead of an internal error or a parse failure naming the stack; a Fraction power past 1,000,000 digits (`2^(10^7)`) fails with `result_too_large` ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/50)).
- **`CALC_MAX_EXPRESSION_LENGTH` defaults to 4,000** code units (was 1,000) and the `expression` description states it ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/50)).

## Fixed

- **`std` and `variance` with a dimension and a normalization read the dimension one-based**, as `mean` does: `variance([1, 2; 3, 4], 1, "uncorrected")` returns `[1, 1]` (was `[0.25, 0.25]`; [mathjs#3779](https://github.com/josdejong/mathjs/issues/3779)).
- **The `range` guard counts what a call builds**: `range(2000000, 1)` returns `[]`, end-exclusive ranges of exactly 1,000,000 elements evaluate, and a zero step fails with `evaluation_failed` ([#43](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/43)).
- **Fraction powers with an irrational value** (`2^(1/10^30)`) fail with `fraction_unsupported` instead of passing as exact when their 64-bit float rounds to an integer ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/calculator-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **BigNumber recovery hints** no longer suggest BigNumber for a failure it cannot fix: a float of more than 15 digits points to `bignumber(…)`, and `erf`, `lgamma`, and `zeta` point to `"number"`.
- **Code in recovery hints** renders as code spans in `content[]`; `structuredContent` is unchanged.

## Security

- **Per-call limits**: `range` builds at most 1,000,000 elements, `stirlingS2` and `bellNumbers` take n up to 1,412 (314 with a BigNumber argument), and `bernoulli` takes an even n up to 1,000; each fails with `result_too_large` beyond.
